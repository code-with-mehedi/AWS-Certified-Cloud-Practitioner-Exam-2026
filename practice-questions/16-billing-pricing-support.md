### Question 1

Which AWS Support plan is the lowest-cost option that provides 24/7 access to Cloud Support Engineers?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> Developer Support</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Developer provides business-hours access to Cloud Support Associates only.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> Business Support</summary>

✅ **Correct.** The Business tier is the first with 24/7 access to Cloud Support Engineers. New customers now get this tier as Business Support+.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Basic Support</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Basic is free but covers only account and billing questions, with no technical support.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> Enterprise Support</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Enterprise also includes 24/7 access, but it costs more than the Business tier.

</details>

### Question 2

A company wants to categorize and track its AWS costs by department and project across the resources in its accounts. What should it use?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> Cost allocation tags</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Tag resources and activate the tags as cost allocation tags in the Billing console to break costs down by department or project.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> AWS Pricing Calculator</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** The Pricing Calculator estimates the cost of planned workloads. It does not categorize actual spend.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Amazon CloudWatch</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** CloudWatch monitors performance and logs. It does not categorize costs.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> AWS Trusted Advisor</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Trusted Advisor recommends savings opportunities and best practices. It does not categorize costs by project.

</details>

### Question 3

A company wants to estimate the monthly cost of a planned architecture before it deploys any resources. Which tool should it use?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> AWS Pricing Calculator</summary>

✅ **Correct.** The Pricing Calculator models the cost of a planned architecture before anything is launched.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> AWS Cost Explorer</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Cost Explorer analyzes past spend and forecasts future costs based on usage that has already occurred.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> AWS Budgets</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Budgets alerts you when actual or forecasted spend crosses a threshold. It does not model a new architecture.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> AWS Cost and Usage Reports</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** The Cost and Usage Report provides detailed billing data about costs that have already been incurred.

</details>

### Question 4

A company needs an expert firm to help design, migrate, and manage its workloads on AWS. Which kind of AWS Partner should it look for?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> A Software Path (technology) Partner</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Software partners sell commercial software that runs on or integrates with AWS. They are not consulting firms.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> A Services Path (consulting) Partner</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Services partners, such as system integrators and managed service providers, help customers design, migrate, and manage workloads.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> AWS Marketplace</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Marketplace is a digital catalog of third-party software, data, and services. It is not a type of partner.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> AWS Activate</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Activate is a program that gives eligible startups credits, support, and resources.

</details>

### Question 5

Which TWO of the following are generally free of charge in AWS?

*☐ Choose TWO.*

<details><summary>☐ <b>A.</b> Outbound data transfer to the internet</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Data transfer out to the internet is charged.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>B.</b> Data transfer between AWS Regions</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Inter-Region data transfer is charged.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>C.</b> Inbound data transfer from the internet to AWS</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Data transfer in is free. Outbound transfer to the internet is charged.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>D.</b> Public IPv4 addresses</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** AWS charges for all public IPv4 addresses, including idle Elastic IP addresses.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>E.</b> The AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) service</summary>

✅ **Correct.** IAM has no charge. You pay only for the resources that your identities use.

</details>
