### Question 1

A company wants to "go global in minutes" and use serverless architectures to meet its performance requirements. Which Well-Architected pillar do these principles belong to?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> Cost Optimization</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Cost Optimization principles include adopting a consumption model and measuring overall efficiency.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> Reliability</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Reliability principles include testing recovery procedures and stopping guessing capacity.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Performance Efficiency</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Going global in minutes and using serverless architectures are design principles of Performance Efficiency.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> Security</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Security principles include implementing a strong identity foundation and maintaining traceability.

</details>

### Question 2

A company wants to maintain traceability, apply security at all layers, and implement a strong identity foundation. Which Well-Architected pillar do these principles belong to?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> Reliability</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Reliability principles include automatically recovering from failure and scaling horizontally.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> Security</summary>

✅ **Correct.** These are design principles of the Security pillar, along with automating security best practices and protecting data in transit and at rest.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Operational Excellence</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Operational Excellence principles include safely automating where possible and learning from operational events.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> Sustainability</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Sustainability principles include understanding your environmental impact and maximizing utilization.

</details>

### Question 3

Which TWO are pillars of the AWS Well-Architected Framework?

*☐ Choose TWO.*

<details><summary>☐ <b>A.</b> Governance</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Governance is a perspective of the AWS Cloud Adoption Framework, not a Well-Architected pillar.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>B.</b> Scalability</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Scalability is a design goal, but it is not one of the six pillars.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>C.</b> Operational Excellence</summary>

✅ **Correct.** It is one of the six pillars. The others are Security, Reliability, Performance Efficiency, Cost Optimization, and Sustainability.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>D.</b> Agility</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** Agility is one of the benefits of cloud computing, not a pillar.

</details>

<details><summary>☐ <b>E.</b> Sustainability</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Sustainability, which focuses on minimizing the environmental impact of cloud workloads, is the sixth pillar.

</details>

Study: [15.1 The 6 Pillars & Their Core Design Principles](../README.md#151-the-6-pillars--their-core-design-principles)

### Question 4

Which Well-Architected pillar includes the design principles "automatically recover from failure" and "test recovery procedures"?

*◯ Choose ONE.*

<details><summary>◯ <b>A.</b> Operational Excellence</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** This pillar includes principles such as safely automating where possible, making frequent small reversible changes, and anticipating failure.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> Performance Efficiency</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** This pillar focuses on using computing resources efficiently as requirements and technologies change.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Cost Optimization</summary>

❌ **Incorrect.** This pillar focuses on delivering business value at the lowest price point.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>D.</b> Reliability</summary>

✅ **Correct.** Reliability is about performing correctly and consistently and recovering from failures.

</details>

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>B.</b> Cost Optimization</summary>

✅ **Correct.** These are design principles of the Cost Optimization pillar.

</details>

<details><summary>◯ <b>C.</b> Operational Excellence</summary>
